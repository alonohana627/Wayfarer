# Features

What Wayfarer does today, checked by running it. Updated with each feature.

## World

- Hearthvale, the hub town, about three screens long: a waystone plaza at
  the west gate, the elder's townhouse, the inn, a market square with a
  fountain, stalls and bunting, Pippa's shop, a cottage, Brann's forge, a
  tree-top hut, the bakery and Lumi's observatory on the hill, with a hazy
  back row of houses and trees on a parallax layer.
- Five regions of four maps each (two fields, a deep map and a guardian's
  arena): Bellwood Forest, the Glimmerdeep Mines, Murkmallow Marsh, the
  Sunspire Ruins and Frostcrown Peak, each with its own terrain, backdrop,
  decorations, monsters, ambient motes and music.
- Maps linked by portals: walking into a map's end, doors and the waystones
  (entered with up, opened chapter by chapter), with a soft fade between maps
  and a map title that fades in and out.
- A day and night clock (one day in 14 minutes): townsfolk come out or go in,
  windows, lamps and torches light up, the sky, the palette and the music
  change; resting at the inn skips to morning or night.

## Hero

- MapleStory-like movement on GameLib's platformer controller: walking,
  jumping (a shorter hop on a tap), ropes and ladders, ramps, dropping through
  footholds; Speed and Jump percentages scale walking and jump height.
- The hyper jump (taught by Captain Dorran): a second jump in the air that
  bursts forward, or straight up with up held, with afterimages and a streak.
- A three-hit combo, the dodge roll, Crescent Sweep, Starbolt, Tailwind and
  Bloom with MP costs, cooldowns and effects.
- Levels 1-30 on an experience curve; five ability points per level for
  Strength, Agility, Vitality and Magic (or auto-assign) and three skill
  points per level.
- Five gear slots (weapon, helmet, armor, shoes, ring) with five tiers of
  named gear in four rarities, shown on the hero.

## Monsters and guardians

- Twenty species on eight archetypes (walkers, hoppers, chargers and fliers)
  respawning on their runs; elites wear a decoration of their own species and
  drop better loot.
- Five guardians with telegraphed attack patterns, phases and arenas:
  Grandcap, Rumblerock, Queen Murkshell, the Lantern Warden and the Hush.
- Defeating the Hush ends the journey with a sunset scene, the journey's
  statistics and credits.

## Town and story

- Six townsfolk with dialogue trees: Elder Oswin, Pippa, Brann, Lumi,
  Captain Dorran and Nell, some only by day or by night.
- A story in an arrival quest and five chapters, plus side quests (hunts,
  gathering and deliveries) with rewards; a quest log and a tracker.
- Pippa's shop (buy and sell) and Brann's forge (gear upgrades with coins
  and ore).
- Windows for stats, skills, the bag and equipment, and quests; two potion
  quick slots.

## Look and sound

- Chibi anime avatars for the hero and the six townsfolk on one skeleton,
  with the face kit's open eyes and expressions; monsters and guardians share
  the same eyes, ink lines and cel shading.
- Shader-drawn terrain, footholds, ropes, ladders, eight house styles in
  palettes, decorations and parallax backdrops per region; lights and bloom.
- Damage numbers, pickups, level-up and quest toasts, a minimap, a boss bar.
- Synthesized music per region and for the guardians (with night takes) and
  sound effects for every action.

## Menus and saves

- A title menu with three save slots and controls rebinding, with the forest
  played by a demo bot behind it (or `--demo`).
- Saves of the character, the bag, the gear, the quests, the world and the
  journey's statistics, written on entering town and resting.
