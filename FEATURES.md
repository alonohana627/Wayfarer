# Features

What Wayfarer does today, checked by running it. Updated with each feature.

## World

- Hearthvale, the hub town: the elder's cottage, the Sleepy Kettle inn, the
  plaza with five waystones, Pippa's shop, Brann's forge, the bakery and
  Lumi's observatory on the hill, with lamps, trees, flowers and a lookout.
- Bellwood Forest: two fields, the Rootway and Grandcap's Glade; the other
  four regions have placeholder layouts of four maps each.
- Maps linked by portals: walking into a map's end, doors and the waystones
  (entered with up), with a soft fade between maps.
- A day and night clock: townsfolk come out or go in, windows, lamps and
  torches light up, the sky and the music change.

## Hero

- MapleStory-like movement on GameLib's platformer controller: walking,
  jumping (a shorter hop on a tap), ropes and ladders, dropping through
  footholds; Speed and Jump percentages scale walking and jump height.
- The hyper jump (taught by Captain Dorran): a second jump in the air that
  bursts forward, or straight up with up held, with afterimages.
- A three-hit combo, the dodge roll, Crescent Sweep, Starbolt, Tailwind and
  Bloom with MP costs and cooldowns.
- Levels 1-30 on an experience curve; five ability points and three skill
  points per level.

## Look

- Chibi anime avatars for the hero and the six townsfolk on one skeleton,
  equipment visible on the hero; twenty monster species on eight archetypes.
- Shader-drawn terrain with grass caps, footholds with soft undersides,
  ropes, houses, decorations and parallax backdrops per region.
